Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Controller: Eduardo Luis de Medeiros · Netherlands

Viewo is a media player for M3U playlists. It does not provide channels, playlists, subscriptions, or media content. This policy explains what information the Viewo app and this website handle, and the choices you have.

Short version: Viewo has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. Your playlist and playback data stays on your device unless you enable iCloud sync or connect to a source you choose.

Information Viewo stores on your device

When you use the app, Viewo may store the following locally:

  • Playlist names, playlist URLs, and optional guide (XMLTV) URLs you add.
  • Playlist files you import from Files or the share sheet.
  • Channel names, groups, logos, and guide data downloaded from your sources.
  • Favorites, recents, playback progress, and app preferences.
  • Cached playlist and guide data used to speed up future launches.

This information is used to provide the app's features. It is not sent to the developer and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Voice search

If you choose voice search, Viewo asks for microphone and speech recognition access only when you start it. The app uses the audio to recognize your spoken search and puts the resulting words into the search field. When the device and selected language support on-device recognition, processing stays on your device. Otherwise, Apple may process the audio and speech data on its servers to provide speech recognition, under Apple's terms and privacy practices. Viewo does not send voice recordings to the developer or use them for advertising.

Network connections

Viewo connects to the sources you configure and may also load stream, guide, and artwork URLs referenced by those sources. When you enable iCloud sync, Viewo also connects to Apple's iCloud service. Those servers may receive your IP address, request headers, and the content you request, as with any media player. Their operators handle that data under their own privacy policies.

Viewo allows HTTP sources because some user-provided IPTV services still use plain HTTP. Only add sources you trust and have permission to access.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is optional. When enabled, details of remote playlists, including their full playlist and guide URLs and deletion records, are stored in your private iCloud key-value store. Imported playlist files and their library entries stay on the importing device. The developer does not receive or have access to your iCloud data.

Home Screen widgets

Favorites and Recents widgets share display information and channel identifiers with Viewo using a local App Group. The app opens the channel from its local library.

Share extension

If you share a playlist or URL to Viewo, the share extension copies the selected item into a private App Group container on your device so the main app can import it. This handoff stays local.

Diagnostics and TestFlight

Viewo does not include a developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting SDK. If you use a TestFlight build, Apple may collect crash logs, feedback, and usage information under Apple's TestFlight terms and your device settings. The developer may receive that information from Apple to improve the beta.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising. The hosting provider may process standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and delivery. Contact emails you send are handled by the email provider and used only to respond to your request.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you delete a playlist, clear the app's data, or uninstall Viewo. To remove synced metadata, turn off iCloud sync and delete the app's iCloud data through your Apple ID settings. You can also contact us for help.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Viewo does not maintain a server-side account or database about you, so most data is controlled directly on your device. To exercise a right or ask a question, contact us at viewo.support@emedeiros.me.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of the service you request, your consent for optional iCloud sync, and legitimate interests in operating and securing the app and website.

Children

Viewo is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account system or developer-operated data collection.

Security

Data is stored in the app's sandbox and, when enabled, in your private iCloud store. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so protect your device and only use sources you trust.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. When we do, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page.
