How can we help?

Viewo for iPhone and iPad · English support

Viewo is a player for M3U playlists. It does not provide channels, playlists, subscriptions, or media content. If you need help, include your app version, device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Do not send passwords, private playlist URLs, or payment information.

Contact: viewo.support@emedeiros.me
We aim to respond within a few business days.

Common questions

How do I add a playlist?

Open the Lists tab, tap the add button, and choose Add List URL. Paste an M3U or M3U8 URL and save. You can also import a playlist file from Files or share a link or file to Viewo from another app.

Why does a channel not play?

The source may be offline, geo-restricted, using an unsupported format, or requiring a different URL. Try the stream in another player. Viewo uses AVPlayer for HLS/MP4 and MobileVLCKit for formats such as RTSP and MPEG-TS. Some providers block playback outside their own apps.

Why is the guide empty?

The playlist may not include an XMLTV guide URL. Add one in the playlist editor, or set a default guide URL in Settings → Program Guide. Guide data must match the channel IDs or names in your playlist.

Does Viewo include channels?

No. Viewo ships with no content. You must provide a source you are legally allowed to access. Viewo cannot help you find or obtain playlists.

How does iCloud sync work?

iCloud sync is optional. Enable it in Settings → Sync. Playlist metadata syncs through your private iCloud account; imported files stay on your device. You need to be signed in to iCloud and have iCloud Drive/key-value storage available.

How do I remove my data?

Delete individual lists in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall Viewo. If you enabled iCloud sync, turn it off and remove the app's iCloud data through your Apple ID settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is there a beta or TestFlight build?

Beta invitations are managed through TestFlight. If you were invited, install the TestFlight app from Apple and accept the invitation. TestFlight availability may change during development.
