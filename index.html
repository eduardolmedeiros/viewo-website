Features

Everything you need to watch.

Nothing you don't. Viewo keeps live TV focused, fast, and beautiful.

Your playlists, anywhere

Import M3U lists from a URL, the Files app, or the share sheet. Keep as many as you like.

Find it instantly

Search channels, jump back into recents, favorite your go-tos, and browse series grouped by show and season.

Filter by category

Narrow any playlist to a channel group with a searchable picker, a one-tap clear, and search that works alongside it.

Watch your way

List, grid, carousel, and poster layouts with per-list overrides, plus Comfortable, Default, and Compact density.

Movies & series

Play on-demand titles with season and episode browsing, configurable skip intervals, and resume where you left off.

Muted previews

Peek at a channel as you browse — silently, automatically, and at the preview size you prefer.

Plays everything

HLS and MP4 via AVPlayer, with automatic VLC fallback for RTSP, RTMP, UDP, MPEG-TS, and more.

Guide built in

A live XMLTV timeline with channel column, group filters, search, and a now line — plus per-list guide URLs and embedded guide fallback.

Multi-view

Watch up to four channels in a 2×2 grid, tap to choose the audio focus, and expand any tile full screen.

Keep watching

Picture in Picture and AirPlay for AVPlayer streams, plus lock-screen controls and background audio.

Sync & share

Optional iCloud playlist sync and a share extension for importing lists from any app.

Make it yours

Themes, light/dark/automatic appearance, dock styles, English/Dutch/Portuguese, and layouts made for iPhone and iPad.

Bring your own source

Viewo ships with no content.

That's the point. Viewo is a player — not a service. You add the M3U playlists you already use, and Viewo handles the rest: parsing, guide data, favorites, and playback. What you watch stays yours.

  • 1 Grab your M3U link or file
  • 2 Add it from a URL, Files, or the share sheet
  • 3 Browse, search, and start watching

Privacy

Private by design.

No accounts. No analytics. No tracking.

No analytics

Viewo doesn't collect usage data. What you watch is never reported back to us.

Stays on device

Playlists and guide data live locally. They're only sent to the sources you configure.

Your iCloud, only if you want

Sync is opt-in and flows through your own iCloud account — nothing passes through Viewo.
