Features

Built for people who already have a playlist.

Import your list, pick a channel, press play. The rest of the screen stays out of the way.

Any M3U list

Add a playlist from a URL, the Files app, or the share sheet. Keep as many as you need.

Find a channel fast

Search by name, jump back to recents, and save favorites so your usual channels are always close.

Filter by group

Show one channel group at a time, or search across all of them.

Pick your layout

List, grid, carousel, or posters, with three density options. Each playlist keeps its own.

On-demand too

Browse by season and episode, skip in set intervals, and resume where you stopped.

Preview before you commit

See a channel play muted as you browse, without leaving the list.

Handles the hard formats

Plays HLS and MP4 natively, with VLC fallback for RTSP, RTMP, MPEG-TS, and more.

A real program guide

See what's on now and next, with channel list, group filters, and search.

Four channels at once

Watch up to four in a grid, choose which one you hear, and expand any to full screen.

Keep it playing

AirPlay, Picture in Picture, Lock Screen controls, and background audio.

Sync and share

Turn on iCloud sync across devices, or share a playlist straight into Viewo.

Make it look right

Light, dark, or automatic, plus themes, dock styles, and English, Dutch, or Portuguese.

Bring your own source

No channels included. On purpose.

Viewo is a player, not a service. Add the M3U playlists you already pay for or host yourself, and Viewo takes care of parsing, the guide, favorites, and playback.

  • 1 Paste your M3U link or open a file
  • 2 Add it from a URL, Files, or the share sheet
  • 3 Pick a channel and press play

Privacy

Your data stays on your device.

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

No analytics

Viewo doesn't collect usage data. What you watch is never reported back to us.

Stays on device

Playlists and guide data live locally. They're only sent to the sources you configure.

Sync only if you want it

iCloud sync is opt-in and runs through your own account. Nothing passes through Viewo.
